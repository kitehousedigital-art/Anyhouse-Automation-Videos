All fonts here are open-licensed. Instagram's own typeface (Instagram Sans) is proprietary and **not** included; the "IG ..." styles
use the closest free Google Fonts (substitutes as suggested by Kapwing's comparison). Source: github.com/google/fonts.

"Bold" files for variable fonts (Gabarito, Instrument Sans, Lexend, Cormorant, JetBrains Mono, Quicksand) were instantiated at wght=700 with
`fontTools.varLib.instancer`. To add a font: drop a static .ttf/.otf here, then map it in `presets.json`.
